\section{Results}

The frozen analysis contains 960 selected slots on 60 buildings: 934 valid reconstructions, 25 identified model-output failures, and 1 operationally missing observation. Selected completed outcomes number 959. Buildings, rather than descriptions or Builder calls, are the independent units. All aggregate means first average within a building, then weight buildings equally; valid counts are shown with each condition. The strict translation evaluator is server\_v2.2\_strict\_translation.

For O, aligned occupancy IoU is 0.1760 for OpenAI Builder and 0.1772 for Anthropic Builder. Absolute IoU is 0.0762/0.0874, while aligned exact-material-aware voxel IoU is only 0.0210/0.0169. Aligned material-set F1 is 0.4145/0.4451; set overlap does not require the material to occur at the correct voxel. Normalized repair cost is 1.4888/1.8732. The add/delete/replace decomposition is provided in error\_structure.tex. These are counts of voxel operations, not human effort or elapsed repair time.

O is the saved v4 JSON-form visual description record, including human-readable summary, materials, dimensions, elements and rebuild hints. J is a reserialization that preserves the same typed JSON tree; it is not the historical Structured IR. J-O aligned IoU contrasts are OpenAI -0.0057 (95\% CI [-0.0122, 0.0003]; Holm p=0.230677; 175 pairs, 60 buildings) and Anthropic -0.0016 (95\% CI [-0.0092, 0.0056]; Holm p=0.966703; 179 pairs, 60 buildings). Neither provides evidence of improved IoU after the original family correction; absence of evidence is not equivalence. The OpenAI J-O dimension MAE contrast is 0.3352 (95\% CI [0.1138, 0.5630]; Holm p=0.0127987; 175 pairs, 60 buildings), where a positive value is worse. These are separate generation calls, so this contrast does not isolate a formatting-only causal effect.

P supplies partial GT attributes, whereas E supplies the complete discrete GT specification. P dimension MAE is 4.4000/0.0444 and its IoU is 0.1674/0.1691. The paired P-O MAE changes are OpenAI -8.4259 (95\% CI [-9.5463, -7.2100]; Holm p=0.00079992; 60 pairs, 60 buildings) and Anthropic -13.3426 (95\% CI [-14.0315, -12.7000]; Holm p=0.00079992; 60 pairs, 60 buildings). The P-O IoU tests do not establish improved geometry; all effects and intervals are retained in ablations.tex. E valid-only IoU is 0.9219/0.9919, but known-failure-zero IoU is 0.8451/0.7604. E truncation is 5 of 60/14 of 60 for OpenAI/Anthropic, respectively. On common-valid E buildings, the Anthropic-minus-OpenAI contrast is -0.0028 (95\% CI [-0.0105, 0.0062]; Holm p=1; 46 pairs, 46 buildings). Unequal valid-only marginal means do not establish a Builder ranking. E is a conditional diagnostic, not a whole-population upper bound or a pure language-information intervention.

\subsection{Separate exploratory CPU attribute proxy}

A separate, new CPU-derived attribute fidelity proxy was specified before these scores and associations were calculated. It uses strict material F1, coarse material F1, missing-safe dimension accuracy and typed field completeness, with fixed weights 0.30/0.25/0.25/0.20. All 180 descriptions have exact original-source, input and GT hash binding. Missing or invalid dimensions are penalized rather than filled from GT. This proxy is not conventional description quality or a human judgment. All 360 specified associations are retained, including nonestimable strata and unstable intervals; no p-value or Holm family was added. Two Builder outcomes repeat each description. Intervals resample 60 building clusters 2,000 times with PCG64 seed 20261016; this seed is not a decoding seed.

For valid O reconstructions, the composite proxy has pooled Spearman associations of 0.3932 (95\% CI [0.2514, 0.5217]; 177 descriptions, 60 buildings) with OpenAI Builder IoU and 0.3745 (95\% CI [0.2278, 0.5126]; 179 descriptions, 60 buildings) with Anthropic Builder IoU. Using the mean of both Builders only when both are valid gives 0.4183 (95\% CI [0.2662, 0.5488]; 176 descriptions, 60 buildings). Within-building centered associations are OpenAI 0.4033 (95\% CI [0.2204, 0.6077]; 177 descriptions, 60 buildings), Anthropic 0.4042 (95\% CI [0.2409, 0.5761]; 179 descriptions, 60 buildings), and both-Builder mean 0.4478 (95\% CI [0.2607, 0.6358]; 176 descriptions, 60 buildings). These exploratory associations do not validate conventional description quality or establish causality. All five components, rank-centered results, provider strata and failure0 sensitivity records remain in the separate exploratory appendix, including null and unstable intervals. Across the full fixed analysis grid, 144 records have point estimates and cluster intervals, 24 retain estimates without an eligible cluster interval, 12 are undefined because of constant variables, and 180 provider-stratified within-building records are not estimable with one description per building.

\section{Discussion}

The low O geometry overlap and still lower exact-material-aware overlap indicate distinct geometric and material-placement errors in this measured reconstruction setting. A material appearing in the description is insufficient to place it correctly. P illustrates that improved dimensions need not recover voxel layout; E illustrates that supplying the full discrete target can make valid outputs much closer while leaving output-capacity failures. Neither intervention identifies how much information natural language causally loses.

The exploratory proxy associations concern explicitly scored attributes from the same frozen descriptions used by O. They provide a reproducible association analysis, not independent validation of the proxy. Material and dimension proxies share GT with reconstruction metrics, so construction effects can contribute to associations. Within-building centering removes additive building offsets for the complete pairs used; it does not remove all confounding or establish a causal quality effect. All fixed components, Builder targets, provider strata, failure0 sensitivities and nonestimable results remain visible to avoid choosing only favorable associations.

The revised question structure below is post-hoc evidence-based reframing for the paper. It must not be described as preregistered. The original 20 comparisons retain their original families and status; the new 360-record exploratory grid was fixed before its computation and is separate from those comparisons.

\section{Limitations}

The independent sample is at most 60 buildings, from a fixed v4 cohort, with fixed descriptions, prompts, Builder versions, schema and evaluator. The three image-to-description providers (OpenAI, Anthropic and Gemini) have a different role from the two text-to-reconstruction Builders (OpenAI and Anthropic). Repeated Builder outcomes and provider descriptions do not create 360 independent buildings. Valid-only selection, especially under E, changes the estimand; F0 is a bounded-IoU sensitivity for known completed failures only and cannot recover unknown operational outcomes. Failure MAE and repair remain unmeasured.

O/J comprise 361/360 attempts over 360 slots each; the additional O attempt is the approved slot 940 retry. These are single-output calls without controlled repeated sampling. No request explicitly fixes temperature, top-p, top-k, penalties or decoding seed. Available OpenAI response metadata report temperature 1 and top-p 1; Anthropic reports no such sampling fields, so its defaults remain unknown. Response metadata are not a guarantee of deterministic decoding. Fixed model identities and the full safe metadata distribution are retained in sampling\_audit.json.

The original conventional-description-quality question remains NOT\_TESTABLE\_WITH\_CURRENT\_MATCHED\_SCORES. Historical per-sample scores do not establish an exact match to the current 180 records: 40 of 60 Claude dimension records differ, and matched Gemini scores are absent. Historical means and v5 data are not substituted. This does not show that conventional description quality fails to predict reconstruction.

The new proxy scores top-level structured attributes rather than every claim in summary or hints. Dominant-material selection, lexical strictness, coarse categories, storage-bbox dimensions, completeness ties, chosen weights and missing-value penalties are measurement choices rather than validated human-quality judgments. Shared GT, exploratory multiplicity, missingness and selected valid pairs constrain interpretation. Bootstrap dependence correction does not address these validity limits. One-description-per-building provider strata cannot support within-building correlations; constants and unstable bootstrap intervals are reported explicitly. No human experiment validates utility or repair time.

The current fixed 960-slot implementation and evaluator are not identical to historical ARR 200-building, earlier 1,200-run or v5 studies; those observations and scores are not pooled. Example scenes follow a fixed rule but remain illustrations, not population estimates. Additional CPU work can review claim-to-table provenance and alternative transparent proxy assumptions as separately labeled sensitivity analyses; it cannot resolve conventional RQ2 without properly matched, validated quality evidence.
